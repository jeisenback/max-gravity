# Timeline

What happened before day 1 of the game. Dates are in years before day 1. The code and README give events and rarely dates, so where an entry has none, the date is an estimate, confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-08. A `Divergence:` line names an id in `derived.md`.

### time.eros-boomtown

- Source: `README.md` (line 6): Eros Old Town, "the first boomtown, half sealed off now".
- When: Estimate, confirmed: about forty years before day 1.
- What: A boom on Eros drew the first wave of settlers to the inner Belt. Part of the old town was sealed when the claims ran out.
- Who remembers it: Old claim families on Eros and the people who bought their lots.
- Use in scenes: An older hand names the sealed block by its old street number.
- Status: from code (README.md) for the boomtown and the sealing; the date is confirmed (owner, 2026-10-08)

### time.wall-rota-act

- Source: `docs/world/backgrounds.md` (`bg.earth`).
- When: Estimate, confirmed: about twenty-five years before day 1.
- What: The Coalition's coastal arcologies replaced paid sea-wall crews with a rota that every household owes. The wall books date from then.
- Who remembers it: Grandparents on the lower tiers, who remember when the rota was paid.
- Use in scenes: A grandparent's story, told as a debt that was moved from the city to the family.
- Status: confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-08

### time.ring-nine-founding

- Source: `SYSTEMS.ceres` in `js/data.js`: Ring Nine, "a squatter habitat bolted to a gutted ore carrier in Ceres's shadow: four thousand people who could not make Ceres rent".
- When: Estimate, confirmed: about fifteen years before day 1.
- What: The ore carrier was stripped and abandoned in Ceres's shadow, and families who could not pay Ceres rent bolted habitat modules onto it.
- Who remembers it: The founding families, who keep the rota that nobody can explain.
- Use in scenes: A Ring Nine cook says which cargo bay was whose.
- Status: from code (js/data.js) for the carrier and the squatters; the date is confirmed (owner, 2026-10-08)

### time.boneyard-wrecks

- Source: `README.md` (line 6): the Boneyard, "salvagers living in three hundred lashed-together wrecks".
- When: Estimate, confirmed: grown over about twenty years.
- What: Salvagers moored wrecks together at Hygiea, and the wrecks became a settlement.
- Who remembers it: Salvage families, who can tell you a hull's first owner by the lashings.
- Use in scenes: A salvager points at a hull and names the owner who lost it.
- Status: from code (README.md) for the wrecks; the history is confirmed (owner, 2026-10-08)

### time.ceres-water-crisis

- Source: `SYSTEMS.jupiter` (Europa) in `js/data.js`: workers touch a blue enamel pin "when they talk about the crisis at Ceres"; `README.md` (line 36), the Cold Water story.
- When: Estimate, confirmed: about two years before day 1.
- What: A crisis in the water supply at Ceres, which Europa's pumping stations were asked to answer.
- Who remembers it: Europa's pump crews, and anyone on Ceres who rationed that year.
- Divergence: `derived.ice-hauler-opening`. The water economy stays; the origin of the story moves.
- Use in scenes: A Europa worker touches the pin when the Ceres price is named.
- Status: from code (js/data.js, README.md) for the crisis and the pin; the date is confirmed (owner, 2026-10-08)

### time.aquilon-pump-logs

- Source: `js/stories/cold-water.js`: maintenance logs for Ceres cistern pumps, signed off by Aquilon Hydrologics crews.
- When: Estimate, confirmed: within the year before day 1.
- What: Aquilon Hydrologics serviced the Ceres cistern pumps, and the logs for that work are what a fired Ceres water engineer, Mira Castellane, wants read.
- Who remembers it: Mira Castellane, and a recovery agent who would rather the logs were not.
- Divergence: `derived.ice-hauler-opening`. The logs and the engineer stay; the derelict hauler that carries them is the part that moves.
- Use in scenes: A Ceres clerk will not say the company's name aloud.
- Status: from code (js/stories/cold-water.js, README.md) for the logs, Aquilon and Mira; the date is confirmed (owner, 2026-10-08)

### time.hester-mortgage

- Source: `js/captains/hester.js` (`bio`): "the owner, on paper, of the ship she crews, which the bank owns most of."
- When: Estimate, confirmed: about six years before day 1.
- What: After twenty years as a hand on ore haulers, Hester bought the ship she crews with a loan from a bank that holds most of it.
- Who remembers it: Hester, who has the figure by heart, and the bank.
- Use in scenes: Hester names the month the loan was signed, not the year.
- Status: from code (js/captains/hester.js) for the loan; the date is confirmed (owner, 2026-10-08)

### time.tomas-three-hulls

- Source: `js/cast.js` (`CAST.tomas.bio`): "rebuilt the same hull three times for three owners who each sold her out from under him."
- When: Estimate, confirmed: over about twelve years, the last sale four years before day 1.
- What: Three owners in turn bought a hull that Tomas had rebuilt, and sold it. He has not found out who holds it now.
- Who remembers it: Tomas, and the dockyard clerk who keeps the hull's book.
- Use in scenes: Tomas knows the hull by a weld he did and cannot find it on any list.
- Status: from code (js/cast.js) for the three sales; the dates are confirmed (owner, 2026-10-08)

### time.cato-hold-gang

- Source: `js/captains/cato.js` (`CAST.cato.story.left`): "a ship sold out from under her crew, and a hold gang scattered across three ports."
- When: Estimate, confirmed: about eight years before day 1.
- What: A ship was sold while her hold gang was aboard, and the gang were put off at three ports. Cato keeps the watch bill the way the gang kept theirs.
- Who remembers it: The gang, who are still in three ports.
- Use in scenes: Cato writes a name from that gang on the back of the bill every year, on the same day.
- Status: from code (js/captains/cato.js) for the sale and the scattering; the date is confirmed (owner, 2026-10-08)

### time.ines-landing

- Source: `js/cast.js` (`CAST.ines.bio`): "the night she put a failing shuttle down on an unlit pad instead of ditching it as the tower ordered. Forty-one people walked off. She lost her license for the way she did it, and she kept the logbook."
- When: Six months before day 1, after nine years on the ferry (Ines's own intro in `js/cast.js`: "until six months ago").
- What: A failing shuttle landed on an unlit pad against the tower's order. All forty-one aboard walked off. The license board took her license.
- Who remembers it: Forty-one people who do not know her name, the tower, and the board.
- Use in scenes: Ines gives the date of her appeal and never the date of the landing.
- Status: from code (js/cast.js) for the landing, the number, the logbook and the date
