# Titan

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-04. Built on `economy.md`, `work.md`, `jurisdictions.md` and `technology.md`.

## The place

Orange haze and methane rain over the research domes on Saturn's largest moon, under a sky so thick that the light always seems to be late afternoon. Titan's biolabs make the best pharmaceuticals this side of Earth. The scientists are a tightly knit, slightly odd community who work in shifts and eat at the same long tables.

## Who owns it

A pharmaceutical company owns the domes and the labs, and the patents on what they make. It runs Titan as a research station: the scientists on research contracts, with a share of nothing they discover, and the support crews who keep the domes running on service contracts through an agency.

## Labs and domes

The scientists are the reason Titan exists, and everyone knows it. The support crews are everyone else: maintenance, life support, kitchens, cleaning, the hauler dock. They keep the domes warm and the labs sterile, and the company counts them as a cost. Everyone eats at the long tables, the scientists at one end and the support crews at the other, and the middle is wherever the gossip is.

## Living there

The scientists' families live in the lab dome, the support crews' in the service dome, joined by a long tunnel. The children all go to the same school, because there's only one, and are sorted by the time they're twelve. Visitors are loved for the news and the fresh vegetables they bring.

## Growing up here

**A lab family.** A child of the biolab scientists, raised in the lab dome at the long tables, among people who talk about proteins at dinner. They knew the names of molecules before they knew the names of planets. Who is missed: a parent in the labs. Why they leave: they're not a scientist, and on Titan that's the only thing to be. What they hope for: to find something they're as good at as their parent is at theirs, or to come home with news worth the long table.

**A domes family.** A child of the support crews, raised in the service dome, at the far end of the long tables, with a parent who keeps the labs running and isn't on any paper. They know how the domes work better than the scientists do. Who is missed: a parent on the domes crew. Why they leave: on Titan the support crews are a cost, and the agency's contract doesn't go up. What they hope for: work where the person who keeps it running gets counted, or a seat at the other end of the table.

## Assumptions to confirm

- A pharmaceutical company owns the domes, the labs and the patents.
- Scientists are on research contracts with no share in discoveries; support crews are on agency service contracts.
- The lab and service domes are joined by a tunnel, with one school.
