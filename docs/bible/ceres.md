# Ceres Station

Status: draft for the owner's review, 2026-11-14 (#1245). Built on `ceres-coop.md`, `jurisdictions.md`, `owners.md`, `places.md`, `regulars.md` and `powers-people.md`, and on the game as built (`js/data.js`: Ceres Station, the Guild Federation, with a market, missions, a shipyard, an outfitter and fuel). Chapter 1 ends here and chapter 2 is based here.

## What it is

The Belt's busiest port and its largest town: the yards, the docks, the ice house and the co-ops, dug into the dwarf planet's crust. It is where Belt crews sign on, refit, drink and are buried, and where the Belt's guilds sit. Most people in the Belt have family here, or owe someone here.

## Who owns it

The station is a guild station (`jurisdictions.md`): owned by the guilds of the people who work it, not by a company. They sit together as the **Ceres Guild Council**, which sets the berth rates (`economy.md`), owns the docks, the yard floor, the air plant and the rooms, and rents them out: to the co-ops, to the traders, and by the month to the renters of the Warren. The council is Ceres's seat in the Guild Federation (`owners.md`).

So Ceres is run by its workers' guilds, and is not run for all its workers. A guild member votes on the council's rates; a renter pays them. The council owes the finance houses that lent it the money to dig the station out, and in a bad year the lenders' terms are part of what the council votes on, as the co-op's are (`ceres-coop.md`).

## The station

Ceres has almost no weight of its own, about a thirtieth of a g. The station is built for it:

- **On the surface, the yard and the docks.** Pads and cradles on the floor of an old crater, under a lattice of floodlights and cranes: the yard's apron, where ships sit on their cradles (the Ore Runner with her name painted over), and the docks beside it, where a torch ship lands on its pad at very low thrust. Suits and pressurised walkways between the pads and the locks.
- **Under the surface, the rings.** The town is dug into the crater wall: long caverns in the ice and rock, each with a drum turning in it for weight, which everyone calls a ring. The **upper rings**, nearest the docks, hold the council's offices, the guild halls, the market, the clinic and the staff rooms. The **lower rings**, deeper and older and colder, hold the Warren, its bar and its laundries, the ice house's shift blocks, and Kettle's first bar, where the co-op began.
- **Between them, the lifts and the long stair.** A lift car up and down the shaft from the rings to the surface, and a stair beside it that the yard crews use when the lift is slow.

About forty thousand people live here. The rings turn for about a third of a g, which is what Belt people mean by a full weight.

**Its water** is the council's and the Water Authority's (`owners.md`). The ice under the station is cut by the ice house and sold on to the depots; what the station drinks is metered, and dear, and the price is kept high by the people who sell it (water is priced high at Ceres in the game).

## Its law, its trade and its yard

**Its law.** The Guild Federation inspects at the council's lock (`js/inspection.js`). Ceres has a yard, so it is one of the two Guild ports that license machine parts for import (the owner, 2026-11-14; #1205 puts the code right): parts landed without the licence pay a duty. Otherwise the council taxes berth and handling, not goods, and bans little but weapons in the bars (`trade-and-law.md`).

**Its trade.** Ceres makes refined metals, priced low, and wants water, food and medical supplies, priced high; machine parts, electronics and luxury goods are middling (`js/data.js`). The Belt feeds and doctors itself through here, and pays for it.

**Its yard** works at the licensed grade (`technology.md`, The ship's systems): serviced refits on rented cradles, rated parts from the outfitter, and the used parts that come off the Belt's hulls. It is where the Ore Runner is refitted (#1210), and where a co-op can keep a patched hull running for less.

## The co-op's office

Two rooms up a stair from the yard floor (`ceres-coop.md`), rented from the council: a counter, a board with the hulls and where each is, and a back room where the members meet. The share is signed at the counter (`chapter-1.md`, the signing), and chapter 2's meetings are in the back room (`chapter-2.md`, The meeting and the vote). For the last vote, with the lender's officer in the room, the co-op rents the small chamber at the Ice Haulers' Guild's hall, because the back room will not hold everyone who comes to watch.

Kettle, who started the co-op thirty years ago, still comes to the meetings, sits by the door and votes last.

**Chapter 3's openings here** (`chapter-3.md`): the organiser's opens in the co-op's back room after the vote, with the hold's price read out; the master's in the house's Ceres desk, with the charter and the licence signed; outside on the yard's apron, with the hull sold and whoever walks away with the hand.

## The hours

The station keeps Ceres time, a day of twenty-four hours, and the yard works three shifts through it. The rings' lights go down at the station's night, and the Warren's bar is busiest at the change from the second shift to the third, when the yard crews come down the long stair. The market opens with the first shift and the trade floor closes with the second.

## Its people

- **Guild members:** the dock crews, the riggers, the ice haulers and the yard's fitters, each in their guild, with a vote on the council's rates.
- **The co-ops' members**, Kettle's among them, between hulls, in their rented rooms near the yard.
- **The renters of the Warren**, who take the station's work as it comes: the yard, the docks, the ice house (`ceres-coop.md`, Growing up on Ceres). Pax's family among them (`people/pax.md`).
- **The council's staff:** wardens, clerks, the dock office.
- **Traders, shippers' clerks, and Kaur's buyers**, in the upper rings.
- **Main characters with a reason to be here:** Bexa, buying in with twenty years of tug savings; Pax, fighting for a berth (`chapter-2.md`, The cast).
- **The regulars** (`regulars.md`, Ceres Station and the Warren): Dunya Achterberg and Kofi Lenz on the Warren's stools, Mei Tan at her noodle stall outside the co-op's office, Old Bram on the bench at the yard gate, and the others; **the powers' people** (`powers-people.md`): Mirela Sandoval at the Guild's office, Saoirse Brennan at Kaur's buying office.

## How it reads

Ceres is the Belt's own place: not the owners', and not fair either. Everyone here works for someone, the guilds look after their members and not much further, and the Warren knows exactly which co-ops hire from it. It is loud, crowded, cold in the lower rings and proud of itself. When a co-op hull comes in, the Warren's bar sings it down the long stair.
