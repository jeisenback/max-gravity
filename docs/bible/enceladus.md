# Enceladus

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-04. Built on `economy.md`, `work.md`, `jurisdictions.md` and `technology.md`.

## The place

Ice geysers blasting into space in tall white plumes off Saturn's small moon, catching the light of a distant Sun. Haulers queue for hours to scoop the purest water in the system, drifting in loose orbit while their crews watch the plumes and swap news over the radio. There is a small dock crew, a smaller shop, and a great deal of quiet.

## Who owns it

A water company holds the scooping licence and sells slots in the queue: a hauler buys a slot, waits its turn in orbit, and scoops a set tonnage from the plume. The company keeps a small dock on the surface for refits, the slot office and the shop, and a dock crew of a few families on long contracts.

## The dock and the queue

The dock crew run the slot office and the dock, refit the scoops, and keep the queue in order. They're a handful of families who've been there for decades and talk very little. The haulers in the queue are mostly small family ships, working the run between Enceladus and the inner system, spending days at a time in orbit waiting their turn, and landing at the dock only for a refit or the shop.

## Living there

The dock families live in a few modules by the slot office, with a window onto the plumes. The hauler families live aboard, and their children grow up in loose orbit, watching the geysers through the port, talking to other hauler children over the radio, and going down to the dock a few hours at a time.

## Growing up here

**A dock family.** A child of one of the dock crew families, raised in the modules by the slot office, with the plumes outside the window and a few dozen people in the whole world. They can tell a hauler by its scoop before it's in range. Who is missed: a parent in the slot office. Why they leave: there's nobody their age on the surface, and the contract only has room for the families on it. What they hope for: to find out what it's like where it's loud, or to come back to the quiet and keep the dock.

**A queue family.** A child of a hauler family working the queue, raised aboard in loose orbit, waiting days for a slot with the plumes in the port, and talking to the other queue children over the radio for hours. They've been to the dock a hundred times and never stayed the night. Who is missed: a parent still on the family hauler. Why they leave: the slot prices went up and the family ship can only carry so many. What they hope for: a slot of the family's own on a good contract, or a ship that doesn't have to wait.
