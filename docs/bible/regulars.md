## The rule

A regular is a place's person: always at their place, known by the second visit, with a light life that moves between visits (`js/regulars.js`: two at each bar, an arc of three beats told as their news, a regular who is gone gives up the stool). Today they are generated at landing from the port's culture. After this page:

- **Each port has a written pool**, about twice its live places (`cast.md`, the writing budget). A live place is somewhere the hand meets a regular: a bar stool, a stall, a desk, a bench.
- **Each run draws a share of it**, seeded by the run and the port, so a port's faces differ between runs and stay the same within one.
- **Written regulars take their places first**; generated ones fill what is left, with the library of arcs as now (`ARCS`).
- **A written regular has their own arc**, three beats, ending `gone` (they leave the place, and may turn up elsewhere, a thread away) or `stays`.
- **The dock official** stays one per port and is the same face every landing (`js/official.js`); their name is still generated, and the port's page gives their manner.

Each entry below is: who, where they are, what they want, and their arc in three beats.
