# The powers' people

Status: draft for the owner's review, 2026-11-14 (#1213). Step 2 of the writing order in `cast.md`: a recurring pool for each minor power the hand meets in chapter 1, so that chapter 2 knows them. The powers are `owners.md`'s; the arcs that reach them are `chapter-1.md`'s (Where the hand reaches the world). The major powers' people come with chapter 2 (#1220).

## The rule

Each power the hand meets has a few named people: who they are, where they appear, what they want, and whose they are. They are **recurring** (`cast.md`): a short thread, two or three beats, that leaves and comes back, shaped by the marks the hand left on their power (the arcs' outcomes, the strike, the record). A pool holds about half again as many as a run meets, so not every run meets the same faces; the arcs' contacts are always met, since the arcs are written around them.

Unnamed kinds in `owners.md` that a pool needs are named here.
