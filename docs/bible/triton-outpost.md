# Triton Outpost

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-04. Built on `economy.md`, `work.md`, `jurisdictions.md` and `technology.md`.

## The place

The edge of human space: a handful of pressurised domes on Triton, weeks from anywhere by torch and four hours from anyone by message. The settlers cut ice and some ore, which ships out by slow freighter, and they buy everything they can't make: parts, medicine, seed stock, people.

## Who owns it

The settlers' association owns the domes, which it bought from a survey company that went under, with a loan from a finance house it still pays. Everything the outpost needs comes in on one carrier's supply ships under a contract the association signs every five years, and the carrier sets most of the terms, because nobody else will run that far out. The settlers measure time by the supply ships.

## The supply crews

The carrier's supply ships make the run a few times a year, and many of their crews are families who live aboard. Their children are raised on the run, and spend a few weeks every few months on Triton, where they have friends, a bunk and nothing else. The settlers know them by name and feed them well. They are guests.

## Living there

The settlers live in the domes, close together, a few hundred families who know everything about each other. Nobody's ever lonely for long, and nobody has any privacy. A child sent away for school or work goes for years, because the trip is weeks and costs more than a season's wages.

## Growing up here

**In the domes.** A child of a settler family, raised in a few hundred square metres of dome with the same families all their life, counting the year by the supply ships. They know every face on Triton and very few anywhere else. Who is missed: a parent who works the ice. Why they leave: the outpost needs what only money from outside can buy, and someone in every family goes to earn it. What they hope for: to send home enough for the next contract, or to see one place where the sky isn't dark.

**On the supply run.** A child of a supply-ship crew, raised aboard on the run out to Triton and back, a guest in the domes every few months and at home nowhere but the ship. They've seen more of the system than most settlers ever will, and stayed in none of it. Who is missed: a parent still crewing the supply run. Why they leave: there's no berth for them on the family's ship. What they hope for: a place they don't have to leave in three weeks, or a berth of their own on the run.
