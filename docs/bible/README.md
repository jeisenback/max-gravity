# The world bible

Status: draft, written 2026-10-31 from the owner's decisions in conversation. It replaces `docs/world/` when the owner approves it; until then `docs/world/` stands where this is silent.

This is the world the game's writing comes from. It is built from the base up: who owns what and who works first, then the jurisdictions that keep that order, then the work, the technology and everyday life, and only then the people. Characters and dialogue come after it and answer to it.

## The owner's decisions (2026-10-30 and 31)

1. **Ordinary life is recognisably ours, a century on.** People talk the way people talk now. The space parts are their normal, mentioned the way we mention trains.
2. **No great powers.** The system is a patchwork of owned jurisdictions: corporate spaces that run across planets, Earth's arcologies, Mars's domes, Belt co-ops and guild stations, and the squats and pirate havens in the gaps. The game's factions will likely become major and minor ones.
3. **The conflict is rivalry among owners** for the frontier's ports, fuel, lift off Earth, mines and refineries.
4. **Earth's nations are gone.** The arcologies replaced them as city-states run by their holding companies.
5. **Only owners own. Everyone else rents everything:** housing, air, water, tools, software, design files, licences, medicine. Hester rents her ship.
6. **Crews are hired like today's merchant navy:** cheap registries, manning agencies, recruitment debt, deductions from pay.
7. **Technology:** torch drives are the one big leap. Otherwise an aging fleet, software everyone grumbles about, light-speed lag that shapes family life, and technology spread unevenly by class.

## How to read it

Each section says what is true, then what follows from it in everyday life. Each ends with **Assumptions to confirm**: things this draft decided that the owner has not.

The voice that uses this world is in `docs/bible/voice.md` (to come). In short: plain, ordinary speech; Brechtian distance in the narration, where who owns, charges and pays is stated flatly and never commented on; extraction shown by setting facts side by side; no villains, only the system working as it was built to.
