# Juno Commons

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-04. Built on `economy.md`, `work.md` and `jurisdictions.md`.

## The place

A spun-up rock at Juno, three thousand families and their greenhouses. The Commons grows food for itself and sells the rest, fresh, to the stations around it that can't grow their own. Every cabin has a window box and every corridor has an herb bed, because the Commons rules say so.

## Who owns it

The growers' co-op owns the rock, the spin and the greenhouses. A member family holds a share and a greenhouse, and the greenhouse passes to its children. The co-op owes the finance house that lent it the money for the spin, and pays it from the produce sales; when the price of food falls, the lender sets the terms. Non-members rent cabins from the co-op and work in members' greenhouses for a wage.

## The incomers

Juno took in families from Ceres when the rents there rose, a generation ago, and has taken more every bad year since. The incomers rent. The co-op's rules let a renting family buy a share when one comes up, at a price the members vote on, and few come up. The incomers are welcome, fed, and still called the new families twenty years on.

## Living there

The member families live along the greenhouse rings, the incomers in the cabins nearer the hub, where the spin is lighter and the cabins are smaller. The elders hold court in the shade of the lemon trees in the oldest greenhouse and remember every ship that ever put in. Everyone knows everyone, and everyone knows who rents.

## Growing up here

**In a greenhouse family.** A child of a member family with a greenhouse, raised along the rings in full spin, working the family's beds from the age they could carry a tray. They know the elders by name and the elders know them. Who is missed: a grandparent who keeps the family's greenhouse. Why they leave: the greenhouse goes to one child, and they're not the one. What they hope for: a greenhouse of their own somewhere, or to come home with something the Commons can't grow.

**In an incomer family.** A child of a family that came from Ceres when the rents rose, raised in a small cabin near the hub, working in a member's greenhouse for a wage. They were born on Juno and are still from one of the new families. Who is missed: a parent who works someone else's beds. Why they leave: the share the family's waiting for isn't coming up, and the wage won't buy it if it does. What they hope for: the family's share, or a place where nobody asks how long they've been there.
