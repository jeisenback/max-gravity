# The Rook

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-04. Built on `economy.md`, `work.md` and `jurisdictions.md`.

## The place

A hollowed-out rock at Hygiea that nobody officially admits exists, with a dock that answers to no flag and signs in a dozen languages that all say the same thing: no weapons, no trouble. Stolen goods go cheap here, if you can get them out. The bartenders are very polite.

## Who owns it

The dock authority runs the Rook, and the dock authority is a few families who've held it for three generations. They charge berth fees, keep the peace and take a cut of every deal made on the dock. They ask no questions about cargo or registry, require no insurance, and keep no records anyone outside could subpoena. Owners elsewhere know the Rook is where their stolen cargo goes, and leave it alone because it's also where they buy back what they need without paperwork.

## The dock, and the market

The dock families keep the peace: no weapons past the lock, and no trouble anywhere on the rock, enforced by the dock's own people, who are polite until the moment they aren't. The market families buy and sell what comes in: stolen cargo, salvage nobody's declared, goods under embargo. They don't steal anything. They take a margin, and they know what everything is worth.

## Living there

The families live in the old galleries behind the dock, the dock families nearest the lock. Everyone on the Rook knows that nothing said there leaves it, and children are taught that before they're taught to read. Ships come and go without names. People do too.

## Growing up here

**A dock family.** A child of one of the dock authority's families, raised in the galleries by the lock, taught the rule (no weapons, no trouble) and how it's kept, which is mostly by being polite. They can tell a ship's real registry from its paint. Who is missed: a parent on the dock. Why they leave: to work somewhere they can say where they're from. What they hope for: a berth where nobody asks, or to go home and keep the dock when their parent stops.

**A market family.** A child of a family that buys and sells on the Rook's market, raised among goods nobody could sell openly, learning their prices before their sums. They've never stolen anything and don't know anyone who hasn't bought something stolen. Who is missed: a parent at the family's stall. Why they leave: the margins are good and the family wants one of them somewhere legal. What they hope for: a trade with a licence, or the family's stall without anyone needing to look over their shoulder.
