# Bexa Oyelaran

Status: a draft for review (#891). Built on `ceres-coop.md`, `chapter-2.md`, `the-belt.md` and `work.md`.

## Where she stands

A member of the Ceres Crew Co-operative, 41, from Ceres Station, with a share in the Ore Runner. Her post aboard is deckhand, and she has the tow and salvage lines: anything that goes out of the lock on a line is hers. She pulled dead ships into Ceres on a salvage tug for twenty years, on a yard's wage, and never owned the tug.

## Money

She put twenty years of tug savings into the buy-in, at the middle figure. Out of what the hull pays her go:

- the rest of her share, about 93,000, like the others;
- the rent on a single room on the station's lower ring, kept while she is away;
- nothing home. There is nobody to send it to.

She has no other savings. Everything she had is in the Ore Runner.

## Family

- **Her father, Tunde**, ran a salvage tug out of Ceres before her, and taught her the lines. He died on one, twenty-two years ago, out on a tow that went wrong.
- **Her mother** left Ceres for Luna when Bexa was small, and they write twice a year.

She isn't married. She was, once, to a dock foreman, and they parted without much noise.

## Who she is

Quiet, steady, and hard to read. She is kind in what she does and gives nothing away in what she says. Ask her a question about herself and you get an answer about the ship.

She keeps a list, in a dozen colours of ink, of the crews she found on the hulls she towed in: a name, a date and a hull number for each. Eleven lived. Thirty-one didn't. Once a year, on the day she found each one, she says their names out loud.

She can tell a hull's history from its airlock. She knew the Ore Runner before Tomas did: she towed her in once, years ago, after a drive failure, and wrote nobody on the list for her. Everybody else gave up on the Ore Runner. That is why she bought in.

She likes: a hull that answers; the quiet of a long tow; people who don't fill a silence. She doesn't like: a wreck left out for the price of the tow; being asked about the list; insurers.

## Aboard

- **Tomas** rebuilt the hull she towed. They talk about her the way other people talk about a relative.
- **Cato** she trusts to run the rota, and lets him.
- **Pax** she watches out for without saying so, and Pax knows.
- **The master** she judges by how he flies the Ore Runner, and by nothing else.
- **The hand** is a member, and gets her vote's respect, and, if they earn it, a look at the list.

## How she talks

Little, and plainly, about the ship. Short sentences. She doesn't argue in meetings; she says what she will vote, once, and then votes it.

Lines in her voice:

- "She's not finished. She just stopped."
- "You tow her home. That's all there is to it."
- "I'll say it once. I'm voting to keep her flying."
- "Eleven. I put the list here for the eleven."
- "Ask me about the ship. I'll tell you anything about the ship."

She doesn't say: the names on the list to anyone, except on their day; anything about her father's tow.

## What she wants

The Ore Runner kept flying, and kept the co-op's. She wants the one ship everybody gave up on brought home and flown for years, and she will vote for whatever keeps her in the air. In the squeeze that can make her the lender's easiest member: a charter keeps the hull flying. She knows it, and it troubles her.

## Changes from the current game

- In the code she is a hired pilot (`role: 'pilot'`, js/cast.js). In the bible she is a member, and her post is deckhand on the tow and salvage lines; Ines is the pilot.
- Her ambition, "a ship nobody wanted", is the Ore Runner itself, and the old promise ("If you ever have ships, I'd like to be asked") goes: she has already bought in.
- Her arrival says "the blockade killed the work". There is no blockade until chapter three (`the-fuel-war.md`); it becomes the yards cutting salvage contracts.
- Her pivot, "The Tow", stays: a hulk to bring in on the line, and a line added to the list.
- "The ghost on the scope" and the wreck detour ("the Patient Wren") stay, as Belt work (`the-belt.md`, tow and salvage).
