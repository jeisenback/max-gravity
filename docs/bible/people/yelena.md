# Yelena Quint

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-07 (#891). Built on `mars.md`, `the-fuel-war.md`, `chapter-2.md` and `work.md`.

## Where she stands

A gunner with Ballast Security, 29, from a hub dome on Mars. A rating on a security company's books, under contract to Tycho Yard Holdings, riding "escort" contracts in the Belt as the owners' war rumbles (`the-fuel-war.md`). She is crewed with people from the same agencies and stations as the crews she stops (`economy.md`), and she knows it.

The co-op meets her on a convoy. Some runs she is beside them, some against them, and she is someone who could be recruited (`chapter-2.md`).

## Money

Ballast pays better than an agency, with a bonus for every contract that comes in without a loss. Out of it go:

- Ballast's deductions for the kit, the training and the berth;
- her family's air account in the dome, which rises every year;
- a surgeon's loan for her knee.

## Family

- **Her mother, Dana**, 58, speaks at the dome council's budget meetings, and has done for twenty years (`mars.md`, the hub domes).
- **Her grandfather, Ivo**, 84, saw the first lake fill. He is the reason she knows the date.
- **Her brother, Teo**, 25, is on a council clerk's apprenticeship and thinks she is wasting herself.

## Who she is

Brave and rude, and both on purpose. Loud, quick, and never sorry. She was a ring-ball striker, and captain of her dome's side, until a knee dislocated in a final at twenty-six and ended it. She took the only other job she could find where you put something exactly where you meant to, and she's still in a bad mood about it.

She captained a team the way she wants to captain a ship: everyone plays, nobody on the bench. Ballast benches people. It sends the ones it doesn't need home without the bonus.

She likes: the ring-ball feed; a clean shot; an argument she can win. She doesn't like: being told to stand down; Ballast's contracts officer; people who quit.

## Aboard

Not aboard the Ore Runner unless the co-op recruits her.

- **Cato** she would like, and argue with.
- **Pax** she would see as a rookie with a good eye and a fear to beat, and say so, loudly.
- **The master** she would take orders from on the bridge and nowhere else; Belanger she knows from Ballast.
- **The hand** she meets across a convoy, and judges by whether they hold their place in it.

## How she talks

Loudly, quickly, and in sport. Everything is a game, a side, a final.

Lines in her voice:

- "A gun's just a very serious ball. You don't look at the thing. You look at where it's going to be."
- "Ballast pays. Ballast pays for you to look the other way, too."
- "I don't care whose side. I care that nobody on mine gets left out."
- "Your ship's slow. Fly closer. I can't cover you from there."
- "My knee? Fine. Ask me again and it won't be."

She doesn't say: sorry; how much the knee still hurts.

## What she wants

To captain a ship the way she captained her side: loud, and nobody left on the bench. Ballast will never give her one. In the war she is a hired gun with doubts: if she sees Ballast bench a crew, hers or anyone's, she can be turned.
