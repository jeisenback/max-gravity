# Tavi Mensah

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-07 (#891). Built on `chapter-2.md`, `the-fuel-war.md`, `the-belt.md` and `work.md`.

## Where he stands

A licensed master, 39, from Ceres Station. One of the four masters in Van Leeuwen & Co.'s pool (`chapter-2.md`), and the youngest. He flew fuel tankers for Kaur Enrichment for ten years, out of Vesta to the small rocks off the lanes, and left when Kaur cut the small runs to save money. The house took him into its pool because Belt masters are scarce.

If the co-op chooses him, he holds the bridge for the co-op and carries out what the player and the meeting decide.

## Money

A master's wage from the co-op. Out of it go:

- his mother's rent on the station's lower ring;
- a loan on a licence upgrade he took to fly bigger tankers, which Kaur then never gave him;
- rounds, more than he should, in every Belt bar he knows.

## Family

- **His mother, Efua**, 64, ran the Ceres fuel office's night desk for thirty years and knows half of Kaur's dispatchers.
- **His sister, Abena**, 35, crews a homeship that works the outer rocks (`the-belt.md`).

He isn't married. He has a lot of friends.

## Who he is

Quick, warm and reckless. He knows the small rocks by name, and which of them is short of what this month, and most of them know him. He flies hard and close, and he is good: he has brought tankers into rocks other masters wouldn't try.

He still talks about Kaur's people as friends, and about Kaur as the firm that let the small rocks go. He will want to run fuel to them when the war comes, paid or not.

He likes: a tight approach; Belt bars; people who say yes. He doesn't like: lenders' letters; long meetings; masters who fly by the book when the book is wrong.

## How he talks

Fast and friendly, in Belt slang, with a joke on top of most things.

Lines in his voice:

- "I know a rock two days off the lane that'll pay double for this. Trust me. Well, trust the rock."
- "Kaur? Good people. Bad firm. You'd like the people."
- "We can make that approach. I've made worse with a fuller tank."
- "Vote however you like. Just vote quick, the window's closing."
- "If the rocks go dry, somebody has to run it. Might as well be someone who knows the way."

He doesn't say: how close the last tanker he lost came; no, to a friend.

## What he wants

To fly the Belt's small runs, and keep the rocks alive. In the war he leans to Kaur, and to the rocks behind it: under him the co-op is the hull most likely to run a blockade, the best paid and the most likely to be shot at.

## Assumptions to confirm

- Ten years on Kaur's tankers out of Vesta; he left when Kaur cut the small runs.
- His mother Efua at the Ceres fuel office; his sister Abena on a homeship.
- He leans the co-op toward Kaur and the small rocks.
