# Pax Iwu

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-07 (#891). Built on `ceres-coop.md`, `chapter-2.md` and `work.md`.

Pax is they/them, as in the current game.

## Where they stand

A member of the Ceres Crew Co-operative, 23, from the Warren on Ceres Station, with a share in the Ore Runner. The youngest member. Their post aboard is gunner: a small hull off the lanes carries a gun for the pirates who work the small runs (`the-belt.md`), and Pax has the best range scores in the co-op.

They are a renter's child, not a member's (`ceres-coop.md`). Nobody in their family has had a vote or a share before.

## Money

The buy-in took everything Pax had saved off the ice crews, and a member spoke for them, so it was the lower figure. Out of what the hull pays them go:

- the rest of their share, about 93,000;
- money home to their mother's room in the Warren, whose rent goes up when the station's owner says so;
- a payment each month on a clinic bill from the coupling, the foreman's, which Pax took on although nobody asked them to.

## Family

- **Their mother, Ada**, 49, works the ice house on the station's lower levels and rents by the month in the Warren.
- **Their brother, Femi**, 17, is on the dock crews and wants a co-op berth.
- **Their father** went out on a contract to Pallas when Pax was nine and stopped writing.

## Who they are

Nervous and curious. Pax asks the question the others don't, and then wishes they hadn't. Quick with their hands, careful to a fault, and hard on themselves.

They ran a drill laser on a Ceres ice crew from sixteen, cutting blocks to the gram. When they were twenty a coupling failed with Pax at the controls and the foreman, Dele Okafor, was hurt. It was nobody's fault, and nobody has been able to convince Pax of that. There is a message from Dele on their slate that they haven't opened.

A co-op berth is what Warren children hope for. Pax fought for this one harder than anyone in the vote: they asked Cato to speak for them, then Tomas, then sat through two meetings to be taken.

They like: the range; a checklist done twice; Femi's messages. They don't like: couplings; being the youngest; anyone calling an accident luck.

## Aboard

- **Bexa** watches out for them without saying so. Pax knows, and pretends not to.
- **Cato** spoke for them at the vote. They would do a great deal for Cato.
- **Tomas** checks their gun's mounts with them and doesn't make a point of it.
- **The hand, as captain,** decides who goes out of the lock, and Pax watches how they decide.
- **The hand** is the member nearest their age, and the one they ask things.

## How they talk

Quickly, and then they stop and start again. A lot of questions. When frightened, very exact.

Lines in their voice:

- "Is that checked? Sorry. I know it's checked. Is it checked?"
- "I don't want the money run. I want everybody back."
- "It wasn't luck. Somebody fitted that coupling."
- "I got a ninety-eight on the range. I want a hundred."
- "I'll vote whatever gets nobody hurt. Tell me which that is."

They don't say: what happened with the coupling, until they trust you; Dele's name.

## What they want

A ship where nobody gets hurt on their watch, and to stop flinching. In the votes Pax wants nobody hurt: they vote against the risky runs, and in the squeeze they are the member most frightened of the war and most angry at being made to fight it for someone else.
