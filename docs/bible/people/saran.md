# Aron Saran

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-07 (#891). Built on `ceres-coop.md`, `chapter-2.md`, `work.md`, `luna.md` and `the-fuel-war.md`. Amended 2026-11-11: the hand captains the co-op's hull, and the lender's masters are met in the war (`chapter-2.md`).

## Where he stands

A licensed master, 54, from Luna. One of the four masters in Van Leeuwen & Co.'s pool (`chapter-2.md`), and the one it sends first to the hulls it trusts. In chapter two he holds the bridge of a freighter under charter to Tycho Yard Holdings on the Belt lanes, a placement the house made when the war began to rumble. The co-op meets him at Ceres and on the lanes, where his freighter carries Tycho's freight past the rocks the Ore Runner works.

## Money

A master's wage, paid by the charter. Out of it go:

- the rent on a flat in the Luna yards, which his wife keeps;
- his licence's renewals and the medicals that go with them, which get dearer every year after fifty;
- a debt to a Luna crewing house from the year he was without a bridge.

## Family

- **His wife, Liv**, 51, is a yard clerk at Tycho Yard Holdings on Luna (`the-fuel-war.md`). She has worked there for twenty years.
- **Their daughter, Lea**, 24, is a third officer on an inner-system freighter.

## Who he is

Upright, tidy, and careful. A good master: he flies safe, he keeps the hull's papers clean, and in a fight he is calm and quick. He keeps his opinions to the bridge, and he knows that what he says to the charterer's agent is in the house's file by the end of the week.

He commanded freighters for Luna owners for fifteen years. His last bridge was sold out from under him and the crew, at a day's notice, to pay an owner's debt; the crew scattered and he spent a year ashore. He took the charter because Tycho pays on the day and his wife is on its books. He would rather have had a members' vote to answer to: a vote is at least something he can read. An owner's mind never was.

Nobody he works with is sure whose man he is: the charterer's, who pays him, or the lender's, who placed him. Neither is he, some days.

He likes: a clean log; a burn on the second; his daughter's letters. He doesn't like: meetings; charterers' agents who come to the bridge to argue; being thanked for doing his job.

## Aboard

Not aboard the Ore Runner. The co-op meets him in port and on the lanes.

- **Tomas** he respects as an engineer, from the Luna yards' talk of him.
- **Cato** he would trust with a rota, and says so.
- **Ines** flies on an endorsement he doesn't like, and he tells her so once, and then never again.
- **The hand** holds a bridge on a registry's paper. He takes them seriously anyway, and tells them where the paper won't be enough.

## How he talks

Formally, in short orders aboard, and carefully ashore. He calls his crew by their surnames on the bridge and their first names in port, and the co-op's members by their first names everywhere.

Lines in his voice:

- "On my bridge, I decide. In your meeting, you do. I'll keep the two apart if you will."
- "I've read the house's letter to you. Mine said the same, with a bigger number."
- "I lost a ship once to a man I never met. I'd rather lose one to a vote."
- "A members' captain. I'd like to see how that goes. I'd like to see it go well."
- "My wife works for Tycho. You should know that. I'd rather you heard it from me."

He doesn't say: what the lender's agent says to him privately; how he would vote, if he had one.

## What he wants

A bridge that cannot be sold out from under him, and his daughter's career clear of the debts he took on. Tycho's charter gives him a sure bridge for now, and his wife a safe job, and he would like the war to end with both. He will not ask the co-op to charter to Tycho. When the lender's offer comes, they will wonder whose side he is on.
