# Ruben Castellanos

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-07 (#891). Built on `mars.md`, `the-belt.md`, `the-fuel-war.md` and `chapter-2.md`.

## Where he stands

A relay keeper in the Belt, 52, from the edge on Mars. He keeps the relays that the small rocks and the freeholds talk on: an open band, on cheap transmitters bolted to rocks off the lanes, kept up by him and whoever he can talk into it. Nobody owns it and nobody pays for it, much.

Kaur Enrichment's tankers find the small rocks through his band: which rock is short, which needs fuel in tanks, who can pay. So Kaur depends on it, and Ballast Security wants it shut (`the-fuel-war.md`). That makes him, to the co-op, an informant, a risk or a cause (`chapter-2.md`).

## Money

Not much. The freeholds pay him in ice, food and a berth; Kaur's dispatchers pay him in cash, quietly, for word on which rocks are short. Out of it go:

- parts for the relays, at station prices;
- his sister's family's share on Mars, in a rationed winter;
- a debt to a Ceres parts dealer, which he means to pay.

## Family

- **His sister, Carmen**, 55, farms the rows in an outer dome at the valley's edge with her husband and grown son (`mars.md`, the edge). Ruben sends what he can when the share is cut.
- **His wife, Lucia**, died eight years ago. They had no children.

## Who he is

Talkative and generous: he gives away what he knows to anyone who listens, and most people do. He ran an outer dome's network for twenty-five years, and knew what every dome was short of that week, who was lying about it, and what they'd pay.

When the domes' patrol shut the open band to break a strike, he kept a relay running in his own kitchen for eleven days, and was let go for it. He came out to the Belt the next year, because out here nobody could shut a band he built.

He believes an open band is how small people stay alive, and he is right about that. He is less careful than he thinks about who else is listening.

He likes: a quiet band at night, with the rocks checking in; a long talk; strong coffee. He doesn't like: closed bands; patrols; people who listen and never say anything.

## Aboard

Not aboard the Ore Runner. The co-op meets him at a freehold or on the band.

- **Cato** he would talk to for hours.
- **Saran** he would not trust, for his wife's job.
- **Pax** he would see as a Warren kid, and give work to.
- **The hand** he meets as a voice on the band, and then in person, and asks for a run: a relay to a rock, or a message nobody else will carry.

## How he talks

At length, warmly, with numbers in it. He tells you three things you didn't ask about and then the one you did.

Lines in his voice:

- "Forty-one rocks checked in tonight. Two didn't. I know which two."
- "They shut a band once on me. Eleven days I kept it up in my kitchen."
- "Kaur pays me. So what? The rocks talk either way."
- "Ballast can't shoot a band. They can shoot me, I suppose."
- "You want to know who's short? Everyone's short. Who's lying about it, that costs extra."

He doesn't say: who pays him, unless asked straight; anything about Lucia.

## What he wants

Every rock in the Belt on one open band, so no owner can starve one quietly. In the war that makes him Kaur's useful man without being Kaur's, and Ballast's target. If the co-op helps him, the small rocks remember it; if the co-op sells him, the lender does.
