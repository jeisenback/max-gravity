# Cato Rahman

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-01. Built on `chittagong.md`, `work.md` and `ship.md`.

## Where he stands

Bosun, 44, from the hill settlements outside Chittagong Arcology. He has been an agency rating for twenty-four years, the last nine on Hester's ship. As bosun he is the senior rating: he runs the ratings, keeps the watch rota, and answers to the captain. He is on the agency's books like the other ratings, at a better day rate, with the same deductions and no share.

He signed with an agency on the port road at twenty and went up as a hold hand on a recruitment debt. He paid it off in his third year.

## Money

What he sends home holds his mother's lease in the arcology. Out of his pay go:

- the agency's cut, every pay day, for as long as it places him;
- the suit and kit he rents from Hester, through deductions;
- money home for his mother's lease, in Chittagong scrip;
- his sister's children's school fees;
- a little into a savings account he doesn't touch.

## Family

- **His mother, Samira**, 70, lives in a two-room flat above a cargo exchange in the arcology's lower floors. The lease is held by what he sends.
- **His sister, Nasrin**, 38, still lives in the hill settlements with her husband and two children, Arif (14) and Tania (11). Cato pays the children's school fees.
- **His father** worked the port cranes, and died when Cato was sixteen.

He isn't married.

## Who he is

Big, calm, slow-moving, and slow to anger. He knows everyone aboard by family: whose mother is ill, whose child has exams, who is waiting on a letter. He writes it on the back of the watch rota so he remembers to ask.

He moves hours on the rota for people when he can, inside what the captain allows, and sometimes a little outside it. Hester counts every hour he moves. He moves them anyway. They have done this for nine years, and they both know it.

He has seen a ship sold out from under her crew, and the hold gang he came up with scattered across three ports in a month. He doesn't talk about it often.

He likes: tea, a lot of it, very sweet; cricket on the relay; a rota that works. He doesn't like: the agency's paperwork; crew who don't eat; being thanked.

## Aboard

- **Hester** he has sailed with for nine years. He is the one person aboard she tells things to, and he keeps them.
- **Ines** and **Tomas** are officers, and he treats them as officers. He gets on with both.
- **The ratings**, the hand included, are his. He looks after them, and expects the work done.

## How he talks

Calmly, kindly, and practically. He asks about people before he asks about the work. He doesn't lecture. When he disagrees with the captain, he says so to her, once, in private.

Lines in his voice:

- "You've got the cold watch. That was me. If you hate it, say so."
- "Your mum's all right? Good. Eat something."
- "The captain counts the hours I move. I move them anyway. She knows."
- "I've seen a gang split up. Three ports in a month. I'd rather not see it again."
- "Tell me what you can't stand, and I'll see what I can move."

He doesn't say: anything that overrules the captain in front of the crew; speeches about the crew being a family.

## What he wants

A member's share in a Belt co-op ship (`jurisdictions.md`): part-owner of the hull, with a vote, so that the people he works with could sign on as members and stay together. He has been watching one co-op's ships for years, and knows what a share costs. It is a long way off.
