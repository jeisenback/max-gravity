A camera on the ship

Both decks, close in, about two rooms at a time. The decks are low: a hand's width over a standing head, lockers and cable runs overhead. The camera follows you and would swing to a room when a scene plays there; the outline top left shows where it looks, and a click on it pans.

Phase
Camera

Upper deck

Berths (Tomas reading in his bunk, a curtain drawn on a sleeper), the galley (Ines and her mug, the cat), gunnery (you at the console, the feed chute to the turret overhead) and the bridge (Captain Vance at the stick, the forward window).

Lower deck

The engine room runs both decks at the stern: the reactor, a catwalk, Oduya at the panels. Forward of it the hold (netted crates), the medbay and the stores (tanks, lockers, a mop that never gets put away).

Outside

The drive plume burns at the stern in the hard burn, thrusters puff at nose and stern to turn her at the flip, and the stars wheel past while she comes round.
