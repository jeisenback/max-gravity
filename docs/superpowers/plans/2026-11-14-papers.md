# The Hand's Papers Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The new-game form becomes the hand's papers (name, home, post, the sheet as four words) with two permanent questions about the life before the ship, whose answers shape the sheet or seed the record, are read back by the sign-on scene, and show on the Record card.

**Architecture:** A pure `papersSheet` in `js/sheet.js` is the one place the trade is computed, shared by the form's sheet line and the game. The form (`js/menu.js`) keeps `home`, `before` and `known` and hands them to `startGame`; `setupHired` (`js/hired.js`) stores them as `st.hired.papers` and applies the `known` answer once through functions that exist (`h.standing`, `recordAdd`, `like`). `youTies` reads the papers' home for the affiliation. The sign-on (`js/signon.js`), the heave-to (`js/engage.js`) and the Record card (`js/character.js`) read `hired().papers`; every new line is in the text layer.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla JS classic scripts, `tests/nodecontext.js` for node tests, Playwright browser tests through `tests/helpers.js open()`, goldens (`UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`), pins (`PIN_WRITE=1`), the text-layer check (`tests/scenelines.test.js` over `tests/sceneplay.js`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-papers-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- CLAUDE.md: read and write `.claude/heartbeat.json`; no emojis; desktop only; classic scripts with unique top-level names; new logic takes state as parameters; an owner is a gate at the edge, never a fork inside; markup on the `h` helpers with a golden.
- `st.background` stays `earth` in the shipped build: the home is `st.hired.papers.home`, and the `starts` scope's forcing in `startGame` is untouched (`tests/scope.test.js` stands).
- The sheet trade is all or nothing: nothing when the point up would pass `STAT_MAX` (5); the floor of 2 is the rule in `papersSheet` though it cannot bind.
- Standing for the shipper answer is `STANDING_GOOD` (2); the patrol answer is one fine through `recordAdd`; the shipmate is a generated crew member (never the cast), any post, drawn with `pick`.
- Words are first drafts in the register of `docs/bible/voice.md`, in the text layer, each played by `tests/sceneplay.js`.
- Commits end with the session's attribution lines; the headless shell suite runs before the push.

## Review Focus

1. A save from before, with no `papers` on the hand: `youTies`, the sign-on, the heave-to and the Record card read `(hired().papers || {})` and behave as today (Task 3 tests `youTies` and Task 5 the card and the heave-to on a hand with `papers` deleted).
2. An owner (no hand): the form shows no questions, `startGame` ignores `papers`, and `youTies` reads `st.background` as now (Task 2 tests the owner's form in the full scope; Task 3 tests `youTies` for an owner).
3. The sheet line and the game agree: the form computes the same four numbers the hand starts with for every home, post and trade (Task 2 compares the line with `newSheet` + `papersSheet` over all 48 combinations).
4. The patrol answer's first heave-to: the officer's line shows once, the fine counts, Marked lands with its moment, and a second heave-to shows no officer's line (Task 5).
5. The shipmate answer with no generated crew aboard (the cast took every post): nothing is applied and nothing throws (Task 3).
