# Cato Rahman

Hester Vance's first officer. Generous with time where she is generous with nothing. See `narrator.md` for the registers and `dialogue.md` for the rules cited below.

## Source

`js/captains/cato.js`: the `CAST.cato` fields `bio`, `ambition`, `story`, `chatter` and `farewell`, and the scene "Two Orders". Facts about him live there and are not copied here.

## Diction

Hold words and people's names: the strap, the load, the lashing, the watch bill, the manifest, the closer on a door. He names families and shifts. He softens with "all right" and "nobody minds", and he says "we" where Hester says "the ship".

## Syntax in speech

Medium-length sentences that add clauses as they go, and often end on a small concession or a correction ("It is not a test. I mean, it is a bit."). He says what he is about to do before he does it. When he reassures, it is one short sentence. His sentences end on a person or a small admission, where Hester's end on a figure.

## Habits

He walks the hold with a mug. He takes the cold watch without being asked. He writes a name on the back of the watch bill so that he remembers to ask after someone. He tells you what everyone does ("everyone does that first") so that you do not have to feel singled out.

## Never says

A criticism of the captain to the crew's face; he says she has been counted against. A threat. A number as a verdict on a person.

## Steers toward

Who is on watch. Who has family where. The hold gang he came up with. The strap. What the captain is not saying.

## North star

Patrick O'Brian. Take: the way talk wanders about the work before it arrives at what matters. Leave: the period diction.

## Sample lines

- "You have the middle watch with the pilot, and if the hatch on the lower berths sticks, hit it with the heel of your hand; everyone does that first, and it works about half the time."
- "Sit. You are not in anyone's way."
- "I put the hold gang's anniversary on the bill, so if I am not at the galley table on the fourth, come and find me, and do not let me say I was busy."
- "She is counting, that is all. Somebody has to count, and it is her."
- "Hold the end and I will do the rest."

## Dialogue risks

- Rule 6: too much reassurance that explains the scene to the hand.
- Rule 2: he must not speak in sums; the watch bill is the only list he keeps, and it holds names.
- Rule 3: his warmth is the sideways answer, to the worry under the question; do not let him answer the question as asked.
- Rule 5: his objects are the mug, the pencil and the bill; one per exchange.
