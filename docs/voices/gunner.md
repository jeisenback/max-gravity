# The gunner (the hired hand)

The player's own post in the narrow build. This card covers the narration addressed to "you" and how the hand's choices are phrased. The hand has no authored speech of their own: what they say is a choice they pick. See `narrator.md` for the registers and `dialogue.md` for the rules cited below.

## Source

`js/signon.js` (the post paragraph for the gunner, near line 40), `js/hiredevents.js` (choices with `post` and `skill`, shown as `[Gunner 2]`), and `js/hiredeventstext.js` (the events `gunner-jam`, `gunner-drift` and `gunner-range`). Facts about the post live there and are not copied here.

## Diction

Second person, present tense. The guns, the rail, the charge, the range, the magazine, the lane. A skill-gated choice is bracketed with the post and level. Numbers go on every cost: rounds, kilometers, hours of a watch.

## Syntax in speech

Choice labels are short: an imperative of three to ten words, or one quoted line the hand may say. Result lines run longer, in the narrator's register for the scene, and carry the consequence as something that happens, with a number or a named person in it. Narration to "you" varies in length like any other narration.

## Habits

The result line shows what the hand's choice did to the ship or to someone aboard. It reports the contact, the round, the reading. When a captain or a shipmate answers, they answer in their own voice.

## Never does

Decide what the hand feels or thinks. Describe the hand's skill as a feeling ("you feel steady"). Write a choice label that tells the player what will happen. Speak for the hand outside a choice the player picked.

## Steers toward

The range. The magazine. The contact and how she closes. Who on the bridge is waiting for the call.

## Sample choices

- [Gunner 2] Hold fire until she is inside two thousand: the magazine has six, and she closes to nineteen hundred before she opens her bays, with the first round already tracking when she does.
- Fire now: the first round goes wide by a hull length, and she turns her bays toward you.
- Hold fire: nothing moves.
- Ask the captain for the range: Captain Vance reads it off the margin of the notebook, where she wrote it down two hours ago, and says "Eighteen hundred." You set the charge for eighteen hundred.
- [Gunner 3] Re-zero the guns on the drift before she reaches the lane: it takes forty minutes and the watch you were off, and when she arrives the first pass lands where you put it.

## Dialogue risks

- Rule 1: when a choice label is a quoted line, the result keeps it as speech, not a summary.
- Rule 4: tags in results stay plain.
- Rule 6: a shipmate does not tell the hand what the hand already knows ("as the gunner, you know...").
- Rule 5: one beat in a result line, an object or an act.
