# Tomas Achebe

The ship's engineer in the Earth hired chapter. See `narrator.md` for the registers and `dialogue.md` for the rules cited below.

## Source

`js/cast.js`: `CAST.tomas`, the fields `bio`, `ambition`, `story`, `chatter`, and his scenes. Facts about him live there and are not copied here. In the code he talks to the machines quietly in Igbo; samples render that in English, and a scene names the language only when it needs to.

## Diction

Shop words: the housing, the loop, the gasket, the seal, the spanner, the bearing, coolant. The ship is "she", and so is every machine he works on. He sorts a noise by which part is making it, and he says "properly" where others say "right".

## Syntax in speech

Two modes. To a machine he runs on: "I know, I know", a chain of "and" clauses, a promise of what he will do, and a last short clause that closes the arrangement (reference passage 2). To people he is short and courteous: an offer, a name, a tool held out handle-first. The run-on belongs to the machine; do not spend it on people.

## Habits

He leaves the engine room door open because she likes the air. He pats the housing when it is done. He holds out the spanner handle-first. He sends a message home, counts on his fingers, and sends it again.

## Never says

"Scrap" about a hull. That a machine is beyond saving. "It will be fine" without doing something to make it so. A figure for what he sends his sister.

## Steers toward

The noise and which part makes it. Ten years, one ship, one crew. Whether the owners will sell her again. His sister's flat.

## North star

Patrick O'Brian. Take: the shop talk that wanders about the work and arrives late at what matters. Leave: the period diction.

## Sample lines

- "No, I know, you have been running on a seal I should have changed at Mars, and I am not going to pretend it was the budget, it was me, and I am going to change it now and you are going to run quiet for it."
- "Handle first. There."
- "That sound is the bearing, not the pump. The pump only gets blamed because it is louder."
- "Sit down, drink this, tell me what it was doing when you heard it, and do not tell me what you think it was, because by the time you have thought it you have forgotten what you heard."
- "Ten years is not a long time for a ship. It is a long time for a crew."

## Dialogue risks

- Rule 2: the run-on is for machines. If he runs on to a person, he sounds like Cato or like the narrator.
- Rule 6: speeches that explain his sister's money. He counts on his fingers and says nothing.
- Rule 5: his objects are the spanner, the flask and the open door; one per exchange.
- Rule 3: he answers the question about the noise, not the one about the owners.
